5. What we are not liable for

6. House cleaning

Our quote is an estimate based on the bedrooms, ensuites, walk-in closets and square metres you give us. If the space is materially different we will requote before starting, and you may accept or cancel at no charge. Cleaning includes: dusting and wiping surfaces, ledges, skirting boards and door frames; vacuuming carpets and mopping hard floors; cleaning ensuites and bathrooms (basin, shower or bath, toilet, mirrors and taps); emptying small bins; and wiping the outside of cupboards and fittings. It does not include: moving heavy furniture or appliances; the inside of fridges, ovens, cupboards and drawers unless agreed beforehand; outside windows or anything needing a ladder; mould, pest, post-construction or renovation cleaning; carpet shampooing and sofa or upholstery cleaning (these are priced separately under Home Services); removal of rubbish or large waste; and handling of valuables or fragile items.
